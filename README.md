# eLearning Fácil — Support · Soporte

**English** · [Español](#español)

Public issue tracker and documentation for the **commercial plugins for Moodle™** by
[eLearning Fácil](https://elearningfacil.com). Report a bug, suggest an improvement or ask a question:
[open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick the plugin.

## Plugins covered here

| Plugin | Component | Documentation |
|---|---|---|
| **Class Manager** — delegated sign-ups with seats under your control | `local_efclassmanager` | [English guide](docs/class-manager/Guide_local_efclassmanager_en.pdf) · [Spanish guide](docs/class-manager/Guia_local_efclassmanager.pdf) |
| **Bulk certificate manager** — issue, download and revoke certificates in bulk | `local_efbulkcert` | Coming soon |
| **Pack Formación Bonificada Pro** — suite for subsidised training in Spain (Spanish only) | `local_efreport`, `local_efcomms`, `local_efdossier`, `local_efalerts`, `local_efready`, `block_eflinks`, `availability_eftime`, `availability_efreq` | Delivered to customers |

## Before you open an issue

- Tell us the **plugin version and your Moodle™ version**.
- **Never post passwords, personal data of students or private URLs of your site.** This tracker is public.
- Licensed customers also have **direct support** through [elearningfacil.com](https://elearningfacil.com), as set
  out in their purchase terms.

## Free plugins for Moodle™

Three of our plugins are free and open source. Each one has its own repository and issue tracker:

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Direct SCORM player](https://github.com/anarosiris/moodle-local_efscorm) | Opens SCORM® packages straight away, full screen, with no intermediate page |
| [Active Time Clock](https://github.com/anarosiris/moodle-local_eftimeclock) | Measures each student's active time on course activities |
| [My progress](https://github.com/anarosiris/moodle-block_efprogress) | Shows each student their own progress in the course |

More at [elearningfacil.com](https://elearningfacil.com).

- Indica la **versión del plugin y la de tu Moodle™**.
